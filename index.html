What is in v0.1.0

Infinite corridor

Chunks streamed around the leader from one run seed. Three lanes, boxes and raised platforms, one lane always open.

Movement and attacks

Per-class walk, run, jump and double jump. Projectiles with ballistic flight, a melee sector for the knight, stamina and mana gauges.

Enemies and waves

Charger, Shooter and Blocker spawn in waves whose difficulty tier rises with play. Damage numbers, death and a results screen.

Rooms and Lobby

Create a room, share the code, pick classes, ready up. A small signalling service in Docker, coturn beside it.

The shared Run

Every player appears in everyone's corridor. Each enemy is driven by exactly one owner. A hit counts once, kills are credited, the leader pulls stragglers forward.

Survives drop-outs

If a player leaves, including the host, enemies get a new owner and a new coordinator is elected. The run ends only when everyone is down at once.

Known limits

  • Models are unrigged, so attacks and jumps show no animation yet. World geometry is untextured.
  • Enemy art is placeholder capsules.
  • The lane Blocker denies a lane through damage pressure, not collision.
  • Multiplayer is verified on a local network. The internet test is still owed.
  • No audio yet.
